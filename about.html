About Harvest&Projects

Creating practical, beautiful and personalised spaces for modern living.

WHO WE ARE

Built on Passion, Precision and Craftsmanship

Harvest&Projects is an interior design and custom carpentry business focused on creating beautiful, functional spaces that are designed around the needs of each client.

From custom kitchens and wardrobes to TV units, furniture and aluminium solutions, we combine practical design with quality workmanship to help transform houses and other spaces into environments that feel comfortable, modern and personal.

We believe every space is different. That is why our approach focuses on understanding what the client needs before developing a solution that fits the available space, style and budget.
